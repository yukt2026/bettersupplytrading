## 4. Strict Image Compliance Verification

- [x] **No Real Logos / Trademarks:** Zero logos from Saudi Aramco, SLB, Baker Hughes, Halliburton, SABIC, or automotive makers (Toyota, Mercedes, etc.).
- [x] **No Fake / Legible Signboards:** All container codes, vehicle plates, and industrial signage are blurred or devoid of identifiable text.
- [x] **No Human Faces:** Photography focuses on industrial equipment, mineral aggregates, and infrastructure to avoid model-rights liabilities.
- [x] **No Implied Asset Ownership:** No imagery suggests Better Supply owns docks, container fleets, mines, chemical synthesis plants, or customer facilities.
- [x] **Disciplined Lighting:** All header images possess subdued contrast and dark color grades ensuring high readability of white overlay text.

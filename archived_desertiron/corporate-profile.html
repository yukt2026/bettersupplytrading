Section 01 Company Overview & Identity
Executive Summary

Who We Are

Saudi presence. GCC experience. Integrated industrial capability delivering coordinated structural steel and multi-discipline construction solutions across the Kingdom.

Engineering Excellence Across Saudi Arabia

Desert Iron is a Saudi Arabian engineering, structural steel, and construction company based in Dammam, serving industrial, commercial, and infrastructure requirements across the Kingdom. Our capabilities cover heavy structural steel fabrication and erection, pre-engineered buildings (PEB), industrial plant construction, pipeline and industrial piping networks, roofing and cladding systems, civil foundations, fit-out works, and associated metal fabrications.

Practical Engineering & Coordination

Led by executive management with more than 25 years of GCC industry experience, Desert Iron combines practical project knowledge, responsive coordination, and a deep understanding of regional construction standards. We bridge the gap between engineering drawings and site realities to eliminate interface risks.

Reliable • Technically Sound • Competitive

From initial enquiry, technical takeoff, and engineering coordination through to fabrication, surface treatment, site installation, and project handover, we focus on clear scope definition, practical engineering solutions, transparent communication, and dependable customer support.

Section 04 Quality Assurance & Safety Standards
Zero-Compromise Standards

Quality Assurance & HSE Excellence

Meeting the rigorous QA/QC inspection expectations and safety protocols required by industrial operators, government entities, and Tier-1 EPC contractors in Saudi Arabia.

01
Raw Materials

100% Mill Test Certificates (MTC) verification for all steel plates, sections, and fasteners before cutting.

02
Weld Inspection

Qualified welders (AWS D1.1 / ASME IX), fit-up checks, visual testing, and certified WPS/PQR procedures.

03
NDT & Testing

Magnetic Particle (MPI), Ultrasonic (UT), Radiography (RT) coordination, and pressure testing for piping.

04
Surface & Coating

Sa 2.5 abrasive blasting, environmental logs, Dry Film Thickness (DFT) testing, and fireproofing compliance.

Domain Governing Standard / Code Application & Scope
Building Design SBC 201 & SBC 301 Saudi Building Code compliance for architectural & structural structural frames
Steel Structures AISC 360 / AISC 341 Specification for structural steel buildings & seismic load framing
Welding Quality AWS D1.1 / AWS D1.3 Structural welding code for heavy steel framing & cold-formed sheet metal
Industrial Piping ASME B31.3 / API 1104 Process piping fabrication, routing, pressure boundaries & inspection
Civil & Concrete ACI 318 / ASTM Specs Reinforced concrete foundations, pedestals, and structural grade slabs
